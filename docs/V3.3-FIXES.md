## 🔧 ROOT CAUSES IDENTIFIED

### 1. **Weak Content Type Detection**
- No validation for product vs article/blog content
- Accepting question titles as product names
- No detection of e-commerce category pages

### 2. **Brand Extraction Failures**
- Extracting single words like "Order", "What", "The"
- Not validating against known brand list
- Poor handling of article title patterns

### 3. **Missing Business Logic**
- No checks for fashion brands (Scotch & Soda)
- No validation that entry is actually a spirit
- Accepting delivery/shipping text in names

### 4. **Quality Score Inflation**
- Garbage entries getting scores of 60-85
- Score calculation not penalizing non-spirit content enough
- No minimum score threshold for storage

### 5. **Category Classification Issues**
- 26% falling into "Other" category
- Fashion items classified as "Scotch"
- Blog posts not filtered out

   OR name ILIKE '%for the ages'
   OR name ILIKE '% shelf reviews %';

   OR name ILIKE 'the top %'
